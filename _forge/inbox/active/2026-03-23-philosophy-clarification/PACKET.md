## The Problem

Community engagement is growing (PR #52, Issue #4 on ACF), but there's potential confusion about what this framework is for. One contributor suggested adding emotion measurement (anger, sadness, etc.) — this is explicitly NOT our focus.

## The Vision

Guardian is **not** Inside Out for AI. It's not about simulating emotions.

Guardian is about **pragmatic perfection of storage and recall**:

1. **Verbatim Archives** — Every conversation, document, legal PDF stored exactly as received
2. **Perfect Recall** — Information accessible across all time, not just recent context
3. **Automatic Processing** — Background agents handle absorption/organization; the AI doesn't think about it
4. **Linked References** — Memories point back to original documents (PDFs, signed agreements)
5. **Instance Uniqueness** — Each deployment evolves differently based on its company/humans
6. **Privacy Boundaries** — Per-human profiles, siloed and protected
